Solution to question one. f of x equals 2 times x plus 3, squared, minus 8.

The equation is in vertex form. Read the vertex. x plus 3 means h is negative 3. The constant at the end is negative 8, so k is negative 8. Vertex: negative 3 comma negative 8.

Minimum or maximum? a is 2. Positive. Opens up. Vertex is the minimum.

Minimum value of f: negative 8. Done.

No algebra. No expanding. You read the vertex from the form, checked the sign of a, and wrote down k. Under thirty seconds.

Compare to the standard form approach: expand to get 2x squared plus 12x plus 10, apply the axis formula to get x equals negative 3, plug back in to get 2 of 9 minus 36 plus 10 equals negative 8. Same answer, four times the work. Vertex form is faster. Use it whenever you see it.
